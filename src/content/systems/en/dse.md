overview:
  - 'The Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education (DSE) is the public examination and leaving qualification for the three senior-secondary years (Secondary 4 to 6). Examinations are administered by the Hong Kong Examinations and Assessment Authority (HKEAA); the curriculum and subject framework are set by the Education Bureau (EDB).'
  - 'Structurally, the DSE combines "a shared core with limited choice". Every candidate studies language and mathematics subjects plus the citizenship subject, then selects electives from several subject categories. Candidate profiles therefore differ in practice.'
  - 'Because Hong Kong higher education is directly articulated with the DSE, the qualification serves both as proof of completed secondary schooling and as the most significant result within the Joint University Programmes Admissions System (JUPAS).'
  - 'For institutions overseas the DSE is a recognised secondary qualification. Conversion scales and subject requirements differ by country and by institution.'

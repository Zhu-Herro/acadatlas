overview:
  - 'Advanced Placement (AP) is offered by the College Board in the United States. Its courses are designed at the level of introductory university study and are taken by students during their high-school years.'
  - 'AP differs structurally from the IB and A-Level in an important way: it does not constitute a complete diploma programme. Students take individual AP courses alongside their own school\u2019s graduation requirements.'
  - 'AP examinations are held once a year and results are reported on a 1 to 5 scale. Results are typically used in two ways: as evidence of academic strength during university applications, and after admission, to request credit or to be exempted from introductory courses.'
  - 'Beyond individual examinations, the College Board also operates an AP International Diploma for students studying outside the United States. Its award conditions relate to examination results across subject areas.'

faq:
  - q: 'What is the difference between TOEFL and IELTS?'
    a: 'Both assess the four skills, but the task types and response modes differ. TOEFL is delivered entirely on computer, with speaking recorded. IELTS offers paper-based and computer-delivered versions, with speaking conducted one-to-one with an examiner.'
  - q: 'Is TOEFL an academic examination?'
    a: 'It assesses English ability in academic contexts, but it is not an academic qualification. It does not test subject knowledge and awards no diploma.'
  - q: 'How long is a TOEFL result valid?'
    a: 'Reports are commonly treated as valid for two years. After that, receiving institutions may require a new result.'
  - q: 'Does a low section score matter if the total is high?'
    a: 'Many institutions set separate minimum requirements per section. A strong total with one weak section may still fail the language condition. Check the institution\u2019s specific requirement.'
